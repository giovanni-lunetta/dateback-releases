⚠️ Important: "My Eyes Only" Memories

                        
Photos/videos in "My Eyes Only" cannot be
                                exported unless you unhide them first!

                        
Before requesting your data from Snapchat:

                            Open Snapchat → My Eyes Only → Select → Select All → Three Dots → Unhide
                                Snaps

                    


                    

                        
Login to Snapchat

                        
Go to the download page and log in.
(You can do this on your phone or computer)
                        

                    


                    

                    

                        
Go to My Data

                        
The link above should bring you directly to the My Data tab.
                        

                    


                    

                        
Select Data to Include

                        
Toggle Export your Memories and Export
                                JSON Files.
Then scroll down and hit Next.

                    


                    

                        
Choose Date Range

                        
Under Choose date range, select All
                                Time.
Confirm your email address, and hit Submit.
                        

                    


                    

                        
Wait for Email

                        
Snapchat will email you when your data is ready (usually 10-20 minutes).

                    


                    

                        
⏰ Time Sensitive!

                        
Download links expire in 7 days after the email.

                        
Download the ZIP and run this app as soon as possible.
If links
                            expire,
                            you'll need to request your data again.

                    


                    

                        
Return to Download Page

                        
Once you get the email, click the link inside or go back to the page above.

                    


                    

                        
Download Data

                        
Under Your Exports, click See
                                exports then Download. Snapchat may split large
                            exports into multiple files — named like mydata~[timestamp].zip,
                            mydata~[timestamp]-1.zip, mydata~[timestamp]-2.zip,
                            and so on. Download each one and save them all to the same folder.

                    


                    

                        
Start Organizing

                        
That's it! Open DateBack — it automatically finds all your ZIP files and confirms the count
                            with you. Click Start Processing to begin.
