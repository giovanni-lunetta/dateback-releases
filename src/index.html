☁️ Cloud Mode Explained

            

                
What is Cloud Mode?

                
This feature processes your memories in small batches (500 files each), pausing
                    between batches to let you upload them to cloud storage and free up disk space.


                

                    
🎯 Perfect For:

                    
    
                        
  • Users with limited hard drive space
    • 
                        
  • Large exports (10,000+ memories)
    • 
                        
  • Uploading to Google Photos, iCloud, Dropbox, etc.
    • 
                    

                


                

                    
⚙️ How It Works:

                    
    
                        
  1. Process Batch 1 (500 files)
    2. 
                        
  2. App pauses and shows a modal
    3. 
                        
  3. You upload the batch folder to your cloud
    4. 
                        
  4. Delete the batch folder from your drive
    5. 
                        
  5. Click "Continue" to process the next batch
    6. 
                    

                


                

                    
💡 Pro Tip:

                    
If you have plenty of space (50GB+), leave this OFF for faster processing.
