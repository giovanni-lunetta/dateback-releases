# DateBack

**Restore the original dates on your Snapchat Memories.**

Snapchat exports overwrite the "Date Taken" metadata on every photo and video with the export date. DateBack fixes that — processing your export locally on your Mac, restoring the real timestamps, and organizing everything into dated folders.

## Download

**[dateback.app](https://dateback.app)** — free for Mac and Windows

Download the right file for your system from the [latest release](https://github.com/giovanni-lunetta/dateback-releases/releases/latest):

| System | File to download |
|--------|-----------------|
| Mac — Apple Silicon (M1, M2, M3, M4) | `DateBack-x.x.x-arm64.dmg` |
| Mac — Intel | `DateBack-x.x.x-x64.dmg` |
| Windows 10 / 11 (64-bit) | `DateBack-x.x.x-x64-win.exe` |

Not sure which Mac you have? Click **Apple menu → About This Mac**. If it says "Apple M1/M2/M3/M4", download the arm64 DMG. If it says "Intel Core", download the x64 DMG.

**System requirements:**
- Mac: macOS 11.0 Big Sur or later (Apple Silicon or Intel)
- Windows: Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit

## How it works

1. Export your Snapchat data from the app or snapchat.com
2. Open DateBack and select your export ZIP
3. DateBack restores the original dates and saves your memories to a folder of your choice
